# Handa Uncle — Bond-market explainer

## Concept
A tactile desk-side financial explanation: paper receipts and investment choices connect to Treasury gravity, then a two-way bond-price mechanism. Ravi's actual recording controls all reveals; graphics teach rather than merely decorate.

## Palette
- Canvas and paper #F7F5F0
- Brand blue #0054AC
- Deep navy #002E5F
- Pale blue #EEF5FD
- Ink #0F172A
- Muted text #475569
- Risk annotation #B42318, only for publication-check labels
- Success #177454, only for semantic directional labeling
Use blue emphasis rather than the generic Vox red. Blue and navy fields may alternate at explanatory chapter boundaries. Shadows use ink with alpha. No neon, gradients or shiny glass.

## Type
Inter 400/700/900 as brand-mandated headline/body font. Space Mono 400/700 for data, dates and annotations. Display 84–140px; giant percentages 200–260px; body 34–42px; labels minimum 24px. Numeric tabular figures. For captions, Inter 700 34px, cream on navy. Keep maximum two short caption lines.

## Layout
1920×1080. Safe side inset 100px, top header 70px, content bottom 810px. Captions in bottom 860–1000px band. Brand logo at upper left; educational attribution upper right. Scene labels and figure markers at edges. No long paragraphs. Stage size full-frame, focal mechanisms toward center/right with short left-anchored explanations.

## Material and depth
Matte cream ledger-paper fields; real generated paper-collage plates with halftone people and blue rough strokes; solid paper cards with 6px blue borders and restrained offset shadows. Do not invent bank/app screens. Official logos flat and unmodified. Generated images need not carry readable text: all labels are real HTML.

## Motion
Narration-aligned staggered reveals, one camera drift per image scene, restrained spring pop with slight overshoot only for collage props, line draws connecting actual concepts, opposing directional arrows for yield/price. Changes continue during longer scenes at word cues. No unrelated idle effects, no auto-playing JS timers. Rules: spring-pop-entrance, split-tilt-cards, svg-path-draw, kinetic-beat-slam (attack variations, speech-driven rather than forcing music timing).

## Audio
Original recording untouched. Clean duplicate WAV 48kHz/24bit, conservative 65Hz high-pass, 6dB denoise, two-pass loudness measurement, target -16 LUFS and -1.5 dBTP. Preserve pauses and timing. Pictory Left Turn music, fade in/out and speech-dependent spectral carve. Generated clip sound effects low below voice.

## Publication safeguards
Recording's year absent. Date-sensitive quoted numbers must be visibly attributed to recording with unverified-date note until creator confirms. No claim of guaranteed USD returns for Indian investors, no expertise credentials invented. Closing stays educational and flags source ending incomplete instead of generating speech to finish it.
